FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does VaaniShield do?

It tells you whether a voice is a real person or generated by AI — voice cloning, text-to-speech or voice conversion.

Which apps does it work with?

Anything that plays sound in your browser: WhatsApp Web, Instagram, YouTube, X, Facebook, news sites, online meetings. You can also upload a file.

How accurate is it?

Error rates of the multilingual detector on clips it never trained on: about 2 % on English, 1 % on Hindi and 0 % on our small Hinglish test set. (The English-only AASIST reaches 0.8 % on English but fails on Hindi.) Real-world audio — noisy phones, compressed WhatsApp notes, brand-new voice generators — is harder, so treat the result as a strong hint, not proof.

Which languages does it work in?

Hindi, English and Hinglish — and 100+ other languages, including Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi and Urdu. The detector was trained on real and fake speech from over a hundred languages. We have tested it most on English and Hinglish.

Why not just use an English detector?

We tried: a top English-only model makes almost no mistakes on English, but on Hinglish it does worse than a coin toss. That is why VaaniShield uses a multilingual model.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. The detector runs on your own computer. Audio is analysed in memory and deleted right after.

How long a clip does it need?

At least half a second; 4–10 seconds of clear speech works best. Longer clips are checked slice by slice.

Can it be wrong?

Yes. Like any detector it can miss a new kind of fake or flag a real voice recorded in poor conditions. If money or safety is involved, always call the person back on a number you know.
